## Books
**Note:** I've included shopping links for reference, but you don't need that specific edition. E-books on Kindle or other E-readers are also acceptable. However, it's **very** important to keep track of important quotes you can bring to class! Including those quotes in your weekly discussion notes is a great way to do that.

* [*Bea Wolf*](https://www.thriftbooks.com/w/bea-wolf_zach-weinersmith/35610797/), Zach Weinersmith and Boulet

* [*Because of Winn Dixie*](https://www.thriftbooks.com/w/because-of-winn-dixie_kate-dicamillo/250433/), Kate DiCamillo
