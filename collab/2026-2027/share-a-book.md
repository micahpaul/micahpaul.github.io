## [Collab Literature](class-description.md)<br>Share a Book Day, 11 November 2026 

We've read several books together this semester. Now it's your turn to introduce your classmates to a favorite book of your own!

Prepare a brief (3-4 minute) presentation about a book you've read and want to share with the class. Come ready to tell us about:
* The author - brief biography about who they were, where and when they wrote, etc.
* The book - title, short synopsis of what it's about (**without any spoliers, please!**)
* What you enjoyed about the book
* Why you would recommend it to us
* What other books it reminded you of
* Anything else that might help us decide if we want to read it, too
