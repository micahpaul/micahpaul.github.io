## Books
**Note:** I've included Amazon links for reference, but you don't need that specific edition. E-books on Kindle or other E-readers are also acceptable. However, it's **very** important for students to keep track of important quotes that they can bring to class!

* [*Pride and Prejudice*](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0307950905), Jane Austen (also at [Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1342))
* [*The Great Gatsby*](https://www.amazon.com/Great-Gatsby-Readers-Library-Classic/dp/1954839243/), F. Scott Fitzgerald (also at [Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/64317)) 
* [*The Outlaws of Sherwood*](https://www.amazon.com/Outlaws-Sherwood-Robin-Mckinley/dp/0698119592/), Robin McKinley
* [*Murder Must Advertise*](https://www.amazon.com/Murder-Must-Advertise-Mystery-Mysteries/dp/0062341650/), Dorothy Sayers (also at [Gutenberg](https://gutenberg.ca/ebooks/sayers-advertise/sayers-advertise-00-h.html))
* [*Adventures of Huckleberry Finn*](https://www.amazon.com/Adventures-Huckleberry-Finn-Mark-Twain/dp/0486280616/), Mark Twain (also at [Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/76)) 
* [*The Humanoids*](https://www.amazon.com/Humanoids-Novel-Jack-Williamson/dp/0312852533/), Jack Williamson
