## [Middle School Literature](..\ms-fall-2026.html)<br>Discussion Questions, 26 August 2026

Remember to bring specific quotes and examples to support your answers!

* *Going Solo* is an autobiography. How does that change your expectations or interpretations of it, compared to a novel?  
* How would you describe Roald's personality, based on the things he does in this book?
* What events or features of his story did you find most: 
  * surprising?
  * interesting?
  * shocking?
* One of the main ideas so far is the concept of building the British Empire.
  * How does Roald describe the various "empire-building" people he meets?
  * Do you get any sense of whether he thinks the British Empire is a good thing? A bad thing?
* The author wrote *Going Solo* about 40 years after the events it describes. Does it seem like his view of the world has changed at all in those 40 years?
