## [High School Literature](..\hs-fall-2026.html)<br>Discussion Questions, 14 October 2026

### *Pride and Prejudice*, chapters 1-29

Remember to bring specific quotes and examples to support your answers!

* What kind of family are the Bennets?
  * Who are the family members?
  * What does each of them seem to want most?
  * What stands in the way of having the things they want?
* What are we supposed to think of Mr. Collins?
  * How did you respond when Lizzie's friend Charlotte agreed to marry him?
  * Is there anything at all sympathetic about Mr. Collins?
* How does Lizzie relate to Mr. Wickham? 
  * What do they have in common? 
  * What do you think of him so far?
* What happens at the Netherfield Ball? 
  * How does it affect Jane? Lizzie? The rest of the family?
* What do you think of Lady Catherine de Bourgh? Does she live up to Mr. Collins's enthusiastic praise?
