## [High School Literature](..\hs-fall-2026.html)<br>Discussion Questions, 26 August 2026

Remember to bring specific quotes and examples to support your answers!

* At the beginning of the novel, Tom is a central character who drives much of the action.
  * How does the tone and feel of the novel change once Tom is no longer in the picture?
* Huck talks a lot about superstitions and religion. How would you describe his ideas about unseen or spiritual things? 
* What kind of character is Jim?
* How do Huck and Jim change between the beginning of the novel and the section where they're on the open river?
* Huck and Jim come into contact with many different characters.
  * Why do you think there are so many?
  * Why do you think Twain makes their stories so complicated and heavy?
* Who are your favorite minor characters so far, and why?
* How would you describe the author's views of slavery?
  * How can you tell what the author thinks as opposed to what the characters think?
