## [Middle School Literature](..\ms-fall-2026.html)<br>Discussion Questions, 21 October 2026

### *The Wednesday Wars*, February-June

Remember to bring specific quotes and examples to support your answers!

* Did anything surprise you in the second half of the novel?
* How do the choices of Holling's sister affect their family?
* What are some ways that we see Holling growing closer to Mrs. B?
* How does Holling continue changing or improving throughout the novel?
* Remember what Holling said about gods dying earlier in the book -- what new "gods" (heroes, relationships, goals, hopes) arise to replace the things that used to be important to him?
* What do you think of the ending? 
  - Do you find it satisfying? 
  - Why or why not?
* What do we learn about Holling as he progresses through the various Shakespeare plays?
  - What does he learn about himself?
