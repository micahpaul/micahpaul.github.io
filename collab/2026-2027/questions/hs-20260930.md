## [High School Literature](..\hs-fall-2026.html)<br>Discussion Questions, 30 September 2026

### *Murder Must Advertise* Chapter 12-end

Remember to bring specific quotes and examples to support your answers!

* What new questions do you have about this half of the novel? 
* Did you find this section easier to read than the first half? Harder? About the same? Why?
* What additional deaths occur in the second half of this novel?
  * Which one surprised you most?
* How does Lord Peter / Death Bredon avoid being killed? 
* Why is the Brothers cricket match important? How does it move the plot forward?
* What new clues do we get about the identity of the killer at Pym's? 
  * Do they become clearer or more obvious as we go?
* What elements of the novel did you find especially surprising or interesting? 
* What do you think of the ending? 
  * Does it feel satsifying? Disappointing?
* Do you think you'd read another Lord Peter Wimsey novel in the future? Why or why not?
