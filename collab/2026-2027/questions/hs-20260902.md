## [High School Literature](..\hs-fall-2026.html)<br>Discussion Questions, 2 September 2026

Remember to bring specific quotes and examples to support your answers!

* The King and the Duke are terrible scallawags. Why do Huck and Jim put up with them?
* How does Huck's battle with his conscience continue in this part of the novel?
  * Does anything change or shift in how he sees slavery or Jim?
* What purpose is served by the long, drawn-out escape plans that Tom, Huck, and Jim participate in?
* Do you read these misguided adventures any differently than you did Tom's original robber and pirate schemes at the beginning of the book?
* How has Huck changed since the beginning of the novel? 
  * What about Jim and Tom - have they changed at all?
  * Do you think they'll behave differently when they get back to Hannibal?
* How does it affect us as readers when we  learn that Jim has been free for most of the events of the novel?
* What about when we learn that Pap Finn has been dead for most of the novel?
* What's your favorite part of this half of the novel?
* At the very end, Huck says he reckons he's got to light out for the Territory so Aunt Sally can't adopt him and sivilize him. Do you think he's serious about that decision?
