## [Middle School Literature](..\ms-fall-2026.html)<br>Discussion Questions, 4 November 2026

### *The Mysterious Howling*, chapters 9-end

Remember to bring specific quotes and examples to support your answers!

* What new mysteries arise in the second half of the book?
* Do we get any answers to other mysteries that have been raised earlier in the book?
* What goes wrong at the Christmas party?
  * Why does it go wrong?
* Does the second part of the book give us any new insight about Lord Fredrick, Lady Constance, and the things that motivate them?
* What do you think of the ending? 
  * Do you find it satisfying? Disappointing? Why? 
* Try your hand at coming up with a wise Agatha Swanburne saying. Be ready to share it with the rest of the class!
