## [Middle School Literature](..\ms-fall-2026.html)<br>Discussion Questions, 16 September 2026

### *The Giver*, chapter 12-end

Remember to bring specific quotes and examples to support your answers!

* Why is memory-keeping painful?
* Why does Jonas stop taking his daily pill?
* What happened to the community's last Receiver?
* How does Jonas feel about Gabriel? What does he do to help him?
* What about the community does the Giver find frustrating?
* How do Jonas's discoveries about the community and Release shape his decision to run?
* How does memory help Jonas to find strength and hope when he lacks those things?
* Do you think the end of the story is hopeful or hopeless? 
  * Why do you think that?
  * Try to see it from the other perspective - what reasons could someone have for reading it differently?
