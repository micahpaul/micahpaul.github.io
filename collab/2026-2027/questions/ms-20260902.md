## [Middle School Literature](..\ms-fall-2026.html)<br>Discussion Questions, 2 September 2026

Remember to bring specific quotes and examples to support your answers!

* Roald tells many stories about being in the Royal Air Force. 
  * Were any of them surprising to you? 
  * Interesting? 
  * Terrifying? 
  * Did they challenge any of your ideas about war?
* What does Roald think about the Vichy French, who are on Hitler's side in the war? 
  * He says that when they ground-strafed a field of Vichy French planes, they all held their fire on the first pass. Why? What do you think about that, in light of his opinion of the Vichy French?
* What do you think about Roald's encounter with the Jewish refugees at Ramit David? 
  * The man says they are going to get their own homeland. What do you think he meant? Can you tell what Roald thinks about that idea?
* What surprises Roald when he gets back to England?
* What did you think was the most interesting part of this second half of the book?
