## [Middle School Literature](..\ms-fall-2026.html)<br>Discussion Questions, 14 October 2026

### *The Wednesday Wars*, September-January

Remember to bring specific quotes and examples to support your answers!

* Describe Holling's family life. How is it different from yours?
* What function do the Shakespeare plays serve in this story? How do they move the plot along?
* What problems does Holling face in this half of the book? 
  * How do they compare to or contrast with Mrs. Baker's problems?
* Holling says, "When gods die, they die hard... They die in fire and pain, and when they come out of you, they leave your guts burned." 
  * What is he talking about? 
  * What gods die in this book?
* Cream puffs are a recurring image in this book. 
  * What part do they play in building the novel's world?
  * What might they symbolize?
* What's your favorite part of the book so far?
