## [Middle School Literature](..\ms-fall-2026.html)<br>Discussion Questions, 9 September 2026

### *The Giver*, ch. 1-11

Remember to bring specific quotes and examples to support your answers!

* How is Jonas’s world different from ours?
* What behavior is required in his community?
* What behavior must be avoided? Why?
* Does it seem like a good place to live?
* Why is the 12-year Ceremony so important?
* What is Jonas's new job, and how is it different from everyone else's?
* What kind of person is the Giver?
* How does Lowry create a sense of strangeness in this world?
