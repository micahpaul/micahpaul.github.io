## [Middle School Literature](..\ms-fall-2026.html)<br>Discussion Questions, 23 September 2026

### *The Penderwicks*, chapters 1-9

Remember to bring specific quotes and examples to support your answers!

* Who are the main characters?
  * What does each of them want or need?
  * How do they pursue the things they want?
* Which Penderwick sister is your favorite? Why?
* Consider a time when one or more character gets in trouble.
  * How does the incident help to move the story along?
  * What does it reveal to us about the characters involved?
* What sort of place is Arundel? 
  * How doesthe setting invite each sister into adventures?
  * How would the story be different if it were set somewhere else - in a seaside motel, for instance?
* What specific concrete details does the author use to help us "see" various characters? Make a list.
