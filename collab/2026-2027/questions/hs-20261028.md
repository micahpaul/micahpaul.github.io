## [High School Literature](..\hs-fall-2026.html)<br>Discussion Questions, 28 October 2026

### *The Outlaws of Sherwood*, chapters 1-14

Remember to bring specific quotes and examples to support your answers!

* What kind of character is Marian? Much? Robin?
  - What does each one sacrifice to be involved with the outlaws?
* How is this story different from other versions of Robin Hood you've heard?
  - What do you think McKinley might be trying to accomplish with these differences?
* Several passages in the book talk about the tales people tell. What relation do the characters' actual adventures have to the tales that are told?
* What stereotypes do we see about Normans and Saxons in this novel? 
  - How does Marian fit in with or challenge these ideas?
* What challenges does life in Sherwood present? How do Robin and his friends meet these challenges?
