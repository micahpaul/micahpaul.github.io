## [High School Literature](..\hs-fall-2026.html)<br>Discussion Questions, 23 September 2026

### *Murder Must Advertise* Chapters 1-11

Remember to bring specific quotes and examples to support your answers!

* What sort of person is Death Bredon?
* What threats and challenges does he face?
* Why is he convinced that this is a case of murder?
* What’s his opinion of advertising?
  * Which opinions seem to be his, and which seem to come from the author?
  * How can you tell?
* What do you make of Dian de Momerie and her "friends"?
* What made this book difficult to read?
* What questions do you have about: 
  * this world,
  * the culture,
  * the language,
  * or anything else?
