## [High School Literature](..\hs-fall-2026.html)<br>Discussion Questions, 16 September 2026

### *Humanoids* Chapter 11-end

Remember to bring specific quotes and examples to support your answers!

* What sets Ironsmith apart from other humans?
* What does Ironsmith want for humanity?
* Jane repeatedly says Forrester is "very good." What is he good at? 
* What scientific ideas in the novel were hardest for you to understand or accept?
  * Did they get in the way of the story for you?
* What is the purpose of the new platinum-based grid? How is it supposed to help humans?
* Does your response to the humanoids change at all from the beginning of the novel to the end?
* Do you think the end of the story is hopeful or hopeless? 
  * Why do you think that?
  * Try to see it from the other perspective - what reasons could someone have for reading it differently?
