## High School Literature<br>Discussion Questions, 19 August 2026

Remember to bring specific quotes and examples to support your answers!

* What are the major incidents or events in this novel?
  * How does each one move the story along or build tension?
* Pick a character. How does the author use each of the following things to paint your character's portrait?
  * Physical descriptions
  * Repeated words or phrases
  * Other concrete details
* Does your character change throughout the novel? 
  * How can you tell?
  * Does our understanding of them change?
* What did you think of the ending? Was it satisfying? Disappointing? Upsetting? Exciting?
* What's the significance of: 
  * Gatsby's many shirts?
  * Doctor T. J. Eckleburg's billboard?
  * The schedule Mr. Gatz finds in Gatsby's old western novel?
* Nick says this is a story about the west. 
  * What do you think he means?
  * What kind of place is the East?
  * In what ways do the characters seem at home or out of place in the East?
* Nick calls Gatsby "a son of God" and says he "must be about his father's business." What do you think he means by that?
