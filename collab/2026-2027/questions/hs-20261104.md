## [High School Literature](..\hs-fall-2026.html)<br>Discussion Questions, 4 November 2026

### *The Outlaws of Sherwood*, chapters 15-end

Remember to bring specific quotes and examples to support your answers!

* Why does Marian go to the Nottingham fair?
  * How does her decision affect the outlaws? 
  * How does it affect the legend of Robin Hood?
* What kind of character is Guy of Gisborne? 
  * How does he compare to Robin and the outlaws?
* What do you think of the ending? 
  * Did it surprise you at all? 
  * Does it feel satisfying? Frustrating?
* What does King Richard seem to want most? 
  * How do the outlaws fit into his plans?
