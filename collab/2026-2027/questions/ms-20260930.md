## [Middle School Literature](..\ms-fall-2026.html)<br>Discussion Questions, 30 September 2026

### *The Penderwicks*, chapter 10-end

Remember to bring specific quotes and examples to support your answers!

* Each of the Penderwicks experiences significant sadness in the second half of the book. 
  * What are the events that bring them this sadness?
  * How does each event change the way the reader feels about the characters and their situation?
* What new things do we learn about Elizabeth Penderwick, the girls' mother, in this part of the book?
* What new things do we learn about Mrs. Tifton?
* One recurring plot device is narrow escapes from varying degrees of danger. 
  * Who experiences such an escape, and how? 
  * How severe is the danger in each case?
* What do you think of the ending? 
  * Does it feel satisfying? 
  * Is there anything you wish was different?
