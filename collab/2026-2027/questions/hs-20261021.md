## [High School Literature](..\hs-fall-2026.html)<br>Discussion Questions, 21 October 2026

### *Pride and Prejudice*, chapters 30-end

Remember to bring specific quotes and examples to support your answers!

* What was your favorite part of the novel? Why?
* What marriage proposals does Lizzie receive? 
  - What are the differences between them?
* How do we come to think better of Mr. Darcy and worse of Wickham?
* What does Lady Catherine's confrontation with Lizzie reveal about each woman's character? 
  - What's most important to them, and how do they pursue those goals?
* How do the various characters change through the course of the novel?
  - Who remains unchanged? How can you tell?
  - Who were your favorite characters? 
  - Who were your least favorite?
 * Do you find the ending satisfying? 
   - Why or why not?
