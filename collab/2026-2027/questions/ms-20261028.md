## [Middle School Literature](..\ms-fall-2026.html)<br>Discussion Questions, 28 October 2026

### *The Mysterious Howling*, chapters 1-8

Remember to bring specific quotes and examples to support your answers!

* How does the author build tension and suspense in the beginning of the book?
* What sort of character is Penelope? What do you find interesting, funny, or sad about her?
* Describe the narrative voice that the author uses to tell the story. How is it different from the narration of *The Wednesday Wars* or *To Kill a Mockingbird*, for example?
* What are some of the mysteries that surround Ashton Place? What questions do you have about Lord Fredrick, Old Timothy, the forest, and other features of the novel?
* Who's your favorite character so far? Why?
